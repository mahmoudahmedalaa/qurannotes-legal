QuranNotes Support

For help with QuranNotes, contact qurannotesapp@gmail.com.

Include your app version, device/iOS version, a short description of the problem and any displayed error code. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or private recordings or reflections unless specifically needed and requested.

Purchases: use Restore Purchases for an existing purchase. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings. Apple handles refund requests. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.

Account deletion: use Delete Account in Settings. If completion is delayed or unclear, contact us. Do not repeat a purchase while the app says it is pending.

Recordings: audio files are stored on your device in this release. Export important recordings before removing the app or changing devices. Signing in on another device does not restore local audio.
