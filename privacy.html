QuranNotes Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

QuranNotes is operated by Mahmoud Alaaeldin. This policy explains how information is used when you use QuranNotes. Contact: qurannotesapp@gmail.com.

Information used to provide the app

  • Account information: Firebase Authentication processes your sign-in information. We use your account identifier and the profile information provided by your sign-in method to manage your account and connect your saved content. Apple or Google also processes information when you choose its sign-in service.
  • Saved content: Notes, folders, bookmarks, reflections, mood check-ins, reading progress and related settings may be stored on your device and in Firebase for account features and synchronization. Reflections, moods and Quran activity can reveal personal beliefs or other sensitive information. Share only what you are comfortable storing.
  • Voice recordings: In this release, recording audio files are stored on the device. Recording information may synchronize separately, but this does not back up the audio file or make it playable on another device. Export recordings you want to keep before removing the app or changing devices.
  • Speech recognition: Follow Along uses microphone and speech-recognition permissions. On iOS, Apple's speech-recognition service may process audio over the network; it is not restricted to on-device recognition. This is separate from saved voice recordings.
  • AI questions and explanations: When you use Noor or AI Quran explanations, your question and relevant conversation or Quran context are processed through our Firebase/Google Cloud backend and Google Vertex AI to generate a response. Questions and responses may be saved for conversation history. Usage records enforce allowances and help prevent abuse. Do not include information you do not want processed by these services. AI responses can be inaccurate and are not a substitute for qualified advice.
  • Purchases: Apple handles App Store payments. RevenueCat and our backend process account identifiers, purchase records and subscription status to verify and restore Pro access. We do not receive your full payment-card details from Apple.
  • Location: With permission, location coordinates are used for prayer times and Qibla. Coordinates are sent to Aladhan to request prayer times. You can change location permission in device Settings.
  • Content delivery: Quran content and recitations may be requested from AlQuran Cloud and EveryAyah. These providers receive the network information needed to respond, such as IP address and requested content.
  • Reminders: Notification permissions let the app schedule reminders. You can disable notifications in device Settings.

Operational data and optional analytics

We use account-linked operational records, such as app activity, purchase outcomes, access checks, usage limits and error information, to operate and troubleshoot the service. Network services also process technical connection information.

In versions with optional product analytics enabled, and only if you choose to enable usage analytics, QuranNotes sends generic app-open, screen, onboarding, reading-engagement, reflection-save and subscription-action events to PostHog in the EU using a random identifier. This does not include your notes, reflections, recordings, questions, answers, verse selections or location. You can turn usage analytics off in Settings without affecting access. Earlier versions with collection disabled do not start collecting simply because this policy has changed.

Account deletion requests include deletion of associated analytics data. Provider processing can take time; the request remains pending until deletion is verified.

We do not use advertising identifiers or sell personal information to advertisers. Service providers process information to deliver the functions described above. The app does not promise that all information stays on the device.

Storage, security and deletion

We use encrypted network connections and access controls. No service can guarantee absolute security. We keep account content while needed to provide the service and until it is deleted, subject to operational records described below.

You can request account deletion from Settings. Deletion can take time while account and provider cleanup is processed. If completion is delayed or not confirmed, contact support. Minimal account-deletion and security records may remain to prevent stale data from being restored and to track completion; these records do not retain your notes, audio or AI conversation content. Apple and service providers may retain records under their own policies. Deleting your QuranNotes account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel subscriptions separately through your Apple ID settings.

You can contact us to ask about access, correction, export or deletion of your information. Keep your own copies of recordings or exported content. Copies you share with others are outside our control.

Children and policy updates

QuranNotes is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information so we can investigate and arrange appropriate deletion.

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. Questions: qurannotesapp@gmail.com.
